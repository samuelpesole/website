## What I do

I'm a **Systems Engineer at Factor-y** (Bari), where I specialise in **Identity & Access Management** and cybersecurity. In practice: I help organisations decide *who can access what, when and how* — and then automate it so it stays secure as people join, change roles or leave. I lead a technical team on our projects and I'm the escalation point when architecture or integration problems get tricky.

Some numbers: 30+ implementations, environments of up to 15,000 users, and a hub-and-spoke architecture spanning 16 companies of the same group. I also sit on the internal team (with our CTO and CEO) bringing ISO 27001 to the whole company.

## What I study

I hold a **Bachelor's in Defence and Security Sciences** and I'm now enrolled in a **Master's in Strategic Studies and Security Policy** (Università degli Studi Link, Rome). My thesis looked at Italy's digital financial system as **critical national-security infrastructure**, in light of NIS2, DORA and the National Cyber Security Perimeter.

The questions that keep me curious:

- How do cyber threats change the way states think about **critical infrastructure**?
- What does European regulation (NIS2, DORA, GDPR) actually change on the ground?
- Where do **technical decisions** (like who holds the keys to a system) become **strategic** ones?
